# Interface symbols

`src/EosIcon.jsx` and `src/DeviceIllustration.jsx` contain original SVG drawings distributed under the project's MIT license. Camera drawings always use a front view. The control symbols follow familiar photographic conventions for single/continuous shooting, self-timer, white balance, autofocus and image quality.

Visual references:

- [Canon EOS R50 manual](https://cam.start.canon/en/C011/manual/html/index.html)
- [Noun Project camera icon catalogue](https://thenounproject.com/browse/icons/term/canon-eos-camera/)

No icon artwork from those sites is included in the repository. Product photos are retrieved from the manufacturer into the user's local cache; they are not covered by the project's MIT license or included in source exports. The runtime prepares transparency for neutral product backgrounds and retains enclosed white details. If a front photo is unavailable, the app displays its built-in camera illustration.
